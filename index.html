About

Hi — I'm Matej. I used to test games for a living; these days I test hardware and software for a different kind of high-stakes system. By night I'm slowly teaching myself how to design the things I used to just break. I like systems that surprise you, stories that stick around longer than the screen is on, and the quiet satisfaction of fixing something that was broken.

Current focus

HW & SW Tester at URC Systems s.r.o., where we build electronic defense systems — surveillance and reconnaissance — for the army. Outside of work, I'm taking a course in level design and slowly turning scattered notes into actual projects.

Experience

QA Technician at Hangar 13 starting late 2022, finding the bugs so players didn't have to. Since late 2025, I've been testing hardware and software for defense systems at URC Systems s.r.o. — a very different kind of "if it breaks, it matters."

Goals

Move from finding problems to designing solutions — level design, game content, the creative side of making games rather than just breaking them. Ship something small but honest, built to the highest standard I can manage, and write more than I currently do.
